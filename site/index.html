DATA & SECURITY

Settings

The website hosts application code only. Your equipment records and photos stay encrypted on this device.

1. Download from CAPWATCH

Enter your CAPID and eServices password. The app requests the authorized CAPWATCH ZIP directly from CAPWATCH to this device and extracts equipment records locally. No data is sent to GitHub or another server.

Android APK connects natively and directly to CAPWATCH. A browser-installed PWA may be blocked by CAPWATCH CORS settings.

Fallback: import a CAPWATCH ZIP already on this device

Use only if the browser blocks a direct CAPWATCH request. Select the ZIP or equipment TXT files. No uploads occur.

No equipment imported yet.

2. Match ORGID to charter number

Manual mapping. Enter the readable charter number for each CAPWATCH ORGID; for example RMR-MT-001.

3. Review erroneous scan readings

Clear only unverified exceptions in the current inventory. Verified equipment remains intact. You can also delete individual exceptions under Equipment → Unexpected.

4. Backups and CSV

Save encrypted backups regularly. Removing the installed app or clearing browser data can delete your local inventory.
